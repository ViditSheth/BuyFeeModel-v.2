# Limitations and next work

- V14 is **directional decision support**, not an exact fee elasticity forecast.
- V11 point prediction accuracy is limited in several historical scenarios; do not interpret empirical V14 ranges as validated forecasts.
- Buyer baseline research is not necessarily event-time evidence for later fee changes.
- Outputs were generated by earlier notebook executions; the new paths do not automatically repair hard-coded local paths.
- The 2026 backtest must rebuild pre-2026 matched-control evidence and evaluate 2026 events without using them for training or threshold selection.
- Do not publish raw proprietary transaction data to a public repository without authorization.
